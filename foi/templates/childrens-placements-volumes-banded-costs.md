# Template: children's placements — volumes × banded weekly costs

Wave 1 request. Deliberately narrow: banded counts, not invoice-level prices,
so the response is a report the authority can run from its placement system
well inside the s12 cost limit, and s43 (commercial confidentiality) has no
purchase — no individual provider's price is revealed.

---

Dear {{authority_name}},

Under the Freedom of Information Act 2000, please provide the following
information about placements for looked-after children for the financial year
{{year}}:

1. The number of children placed in each of the following placement types as
   at 31 March {{year_end}}:
   (a) residential children's homes (own-provision);
   (b) residential children's homes (external/independent provision);
   (c) independent fostering agency placements;
   (d) in-house fostering placements;
   (e) supported accommodation (16+).

2. For categories (b) and (c) only, the number of placements falling in each
   of the following weekly-cost bands as at 31 March {{year_end}}:
   under £1,000; £1,000–£1,999; £2,000–£2,999; £3,000–£3,999; £4,000–£5,999;
   £6,000–£7,999; £8,000 and above.

3. The total gross expenditure for {{year}} on categories (b) and (c).

I am not requesting any information that would identify an individual child,
carer, or the price paid to any individual provider.

If any part of this request would exceed the cost limit in section 12, please
provide the parts that can be answered within the limit and indicate which
part caused the excess, as required by your section 16 duty to advise and
assist.

Yours faithfully,
{{signatory}}
